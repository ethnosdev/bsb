# Demo Video Script & Production Guide

A ~3.5-minute walkthrough of the Berean Standard Bible (BSB) app covering all core features and hidden shortcuts, formatted for a **16:9 YouTube video** (portrait phone screen on the left, feature/shortcut callout panel on the right).

---

## 1. Pre-Recording Setup

Before hitting record, stage a few realistic items so every screen looks rich and natural without awkward typing on camera:

* **Open tabs:** Start on the Book Chooser (or with 1–2 chapters ready).
* **Highlights & Notes:** Pre-highlight 2–3 verses in different colors and add a short personal note on one verse (e.g., in Romans 8 or John 1) so the inline note marker and **Highlights & Notes** manager are populated.
* **Playlist:** Create one sample playlist (e.g., *Sunday Study* or *Psalm 23 Devotional*) with 2–3 Bible passages and 1 short custom note.
* **Reading Plan:** Start a reading plan and check off a couple of days so the **Next Up** card and **Jump to current** button are visible.
* **Touch indicators:** Enable *Show taps* (Android Developer Options) or add subtle tap/swipe animations in editing so viewers can clearly see every gesture.

## 3. Production Script (~3:30 at ~145 wpm)

### `0:00 – 0:30` · Intro & Fast Book Navigation

* **On Screen:** App opens on the Book Chooser grid. Finger drags slowly across a few tiles (showing the full-name popup), then **swipes up** on *Psalms* (jumps straight to Psalm 1). Back out, **swipe down** on *Genesis* (jumps to Genesis 50). Tap *John* → tap **Section Headings** icon on the keypad → pick a heading (e.g., *John 1*).
* **Side Callouts:**
  * `Free · No Ads · Public Domain BSB`
  * `Shortcut: Drag grid to preview names`
  * `Shortcut: Swipe Up = Ch. 1 | Swipe Down = Last Ch.`
* **Voiceover:**

> This is the Berean Standard Bible app—a fast, ad-free Bible reader built around the public-domain BSB text. Let's walk through everything it can do, along with a few hidden shortcuts you might not notice at first.
>
> Starting on the Book Chooser, you can tap any book to open the chapter keypad—or tap the Section Headings icon to jump straight to a passage by topic. Even faster: drag your finger across the grid to preview full book names, swipe **up** on any book to jump straight to Chapter 1, or swipe **down** to jump to its final chapter.

---

### `0:30 – 1:15` · Reading Gestures & Multi-Tab Flow

* **On Screen:** Reading *John 1*. Swipe left to *John 2*, then **pinch** screen to grow/shrink text. **Tap** the text to hide the app bar (distraction-free mode), then tap again to bring it back. Slightly tug horizontally and release (or drag right edge) to reveal and scrub the **verse scrubber**. Tap `+` to open a second tab (*Romans 8*). Tap empty space in the app bar → returns to Book Chooser. Flick one tab chip **down** to close it; then swipe **down** on the empty app bar area to close all tabs (or show the tabs sheet).
* **Side Callouts:**
  * `Pinch anywhere to resize text`
  * `Tap text → Distraction-Free Mode`
  * `Flick tab down → Close tab`
  * `Swipe down app bar → Close all tabs`
* **Voiceover:**

> While reading, swipe left or right between chapters, pinch anywhere on the screen to adjust your text size, and tap the text anytime for distraction-free mode. On the right edge, drag the verse scrubber to jump straight to any verse.
>
> Need to keep multiple passages open? Tap the plus button to open chapters in tabs, which you can reorder or switch between instantly. Here are three quick app-bar shortcuts: tap any empty space to jump back to the Book Chooser, flick a tab chip downward to close it, or swipe down on the empty space in the app bar to close all open tabs at once.

---

### `1:15 – 1:45` · Footnotes & Full-Text Search

* **On Screen:** Tap a word right before an asterisk (`*`) to open a footnote. Inside the footnote, tap a cross-reference link to preview the passage and tap **Open in new tab**. Also tap a source abbreviation (if visible). Next, tap the **Search** icon, type a phrase, toggle **Exact**, and filter by a specific book; tap a result to open it.
* **Side Callouts:**
  * `Tip: Tap * OR the preceding word`
  * `Cross-references open as previews or new tabs`
  * `Search: Exact match + Book filter`
* **Voiceover:**

> When you see an asterisk for a footnote, you don't have to aim precisely at the tiny star—just tap the word right before it. Inside any footnote, tap source abbreviations to see what they mean, or tap cross-references to preview the passage and open it in a new tab.
>
> Over in Search, you can scan the whole Bible, narrow results to a single book, and toggle **Exact** to match an exact phrase.

---

### `1:45 – 2:30` · Verse Tools, Highlights, Notes & Original Languages

* **On Screen:** Long-press a verse, drag selection handles slightly to expand. Tap **Highlight** and pick a color. Long-press again → tap **Note** → save a brief note (show the inline note marker, tap it once to preview). Mention **Copy** (and show the overflow `...` menu with **Add to playlist**, **Compare Translations**, and **Memorize** without leaving the app). Finally, tap **Greek** (or **Hebrew**). Tap an English word → corresponding Greek word highlights with transliteration, grammar, and lexicon entry. Tap **Occurrences** briefly, then swipe left/right between verses.
* **Side Callouts:**
  * `Copy includes reference (unless 1–2 words)`
  * `Overflow menu: Playlist · Compare · Memorize`
  * `BDB & Abbott-Smith Lexicons + Occurrences`
  * `Shortcut: Swipe left/right between verses`
* **Voiceover:**

> Long-press any verse and drag the handles to select across lines. From the bottom bar, you can highlight in multiple colors, attach a personal note—which places a tappable marker right in the text—or copy the text with the reference automatically included above it. And if you only select one or two words, Copy leaves the reference out so you can grab a quick quote. Under the menu, you can also add verses to a playlist, compare translations, or send a verse to Memorize Scripture.
>
> Tap Hebrew or Greek to open the built-in interlinear. Tap any word in English or the original language to link them together, view its transliteration, grammar, and full lexicon entry, tap **Occurrences** to find every verse using that root, and swipe left or right to step through verses.

---

### `2:30 – 3:05` · Audio Bible, Reading Plans & Playlists

* **On Screen:** Tap the **Audio** icon → show playback speed, Sleep Timer, and sync toggle, then minimize to the bottom bar while scrolling. Open the **Drawer**: briefly show **Highlights & Notes**, tap **Reading Plans** (show **Next Up** card and **Jump to current**), then open **Playlists**. Open the pre-made playlist → tap **Presentation Mode** to swipe through a verse slide and a custom note slide → show **Open in Bible Reader tab** (or open a saved playlist as a tab in the app bar) and point out **Copy Share Code** / `.bsbplaylist` sharing.
* **Side Callouts:**
  * `Audio: Speed · Sleep Timer · Auto-Scroll Sync`
  * `Reading Plans: Next Up & Jump to Current`
  * `Playlists: Passages + Notes · Presentation Mode`
  * `Open playlists as tabs or share with a code`
* **Voiceover:**

> Want to listen instead? Open the audio player for adjustable playback speed, a sleep timer, and auto-scroll sync, or collapse it into a mini player while you read.
>
> In the navigation drawer, you'll find all your **Highlights & Notes** in one searchable place, **Reading Plans** with a **Next Up** card to keep you on track, and **Playlists**—where you can combine Bible passages with your own notes. Playlists are great for teaching or devotions: use **Presentation Mode** to step through them full-screen, open saved playlists right alongside chapters as tabs, or share them with a quick Share Code.

---

### `3:05 – 3:35` · Customization, Backups & Outro

* **On Screen:** Open **Settings**. Toggle **Light-Dark Mode**, glance at **Color Theme** (presets + **Custom Theme Editor** with **Words of Jesus in Color**), point to **Book / Chapter / Verse Chooser** options (Grid vs. List/Keypad/Sidebar), toggle **British English Spelling**, and tap **Export Backup** to show **JSON** and **Markdown (.md)** options. Cut back to a clean reading screen in Dark or custom theme.
* **Side Callouts:**
  * `Custom Themes + Words of Jesus Color`
  * `Alternate Grid / List / Keypad Layouts`
  * `British English Spelling Toggle`
  * `Local Backup: JSON or Markdown (.md)`
* **Voiceover:**

> Finally, in **Settings**, you can make the app feel like yours—switch between light and dark modes, pick a theme or build your own custom colors including Words of Jesus, swap between keypad, list, or grid navigation styles, and even switch the text to British English spelling.
>
> Everything stays private on your device, and you can export your highlights, notes, and playlists anytime as a JSON backup or a clean Markdown file.
>
> That's the Berean Standard Bible app—free on iOS and Android, with links in the description below.
